# Volunteer invitation drafts

Prepared for the owner to review and share. These drafts have not been posted to external communities. Use a community's permitted feedback or research channel, adapt the wording to its rules, and avoid unsolicited mass messages.

## For readers, listeners and coloring enthusiasts

**Could you spare 10–15 minutes to help improve an illustrated folklore website?**

Folkloreverse brings together stories, illustrations, audio and a printable coloring sample. We are looking for a few adult volunteers who enjoy myths, audio stories or coloring to try two short tasks and tell us where the website is clear or confusing.

You do not need to know mythology or understand technology. Use your normal phone or computer. There is no purchase, download or website signup required to take part; printing and downloading the sample are optional.

This is an unpaid, one-off test. You can stop or skip a task at any time.

**[Choose your two tasks](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/blob/main/TESTING.md)**

You can leave a public report on GitHub, or reply to the person who invited you through the existing conversation if you prefer. Please do not post personal contact details in GitHub reports.

We are testing the website, not your knowledge. Thank you for helping us make it easier to find and enjoy a story.

## For a UX, accessibility or QA reviewer

**Seeking a short volunteer review of Folkloreverse's navigation**

We would value one or two adult volunteers with UX, accessibility or website-testing experience for a bounded, unpaid review of [folkloreverse.com](https://folkloreverse.com/).

Please choose one flow—finding a complete story, finding and resuming audio, or finding cultural/source context—and spend about 20–30 minutes checking it on your usual device.

Useful feedback would be a short list of specific obstacles, the page and steps involved, what you expected, and how much the issue affected completing the task. If you use assistive technology, observations from your own setup are welcome; nobody is asked to simulate a disability or certify the whole website.

[Tasks and reporting options](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback)

This is a one-off critique, not a request for an unpaid redesign. No source-code, production-system access, installation or purchase is needed. Please distinguish what you reproduced from suggestions or automated findings.

The organizer will review findings before deciding changes. We appreciate observations from real readers as well as specialist perspectives.
