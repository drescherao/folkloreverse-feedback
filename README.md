# Help shape Folkloreverse

An illustrated collection of folklore to read, listen to and explore.

**[Visit Folkloreverse](https://folkloreverse.com/) · [Try two short tasks](TESTING.md) · [Share your experience](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new/choose)**

We want to learn what is easy to find, what is confusing and what makes you want to explore another story. You do not need to know mythology, write code or install anything.

## Take part in 10–15 minutes

1. Open [the testing tasks](TESTING.md) and choose the pair closest to something you would normally enjoy.
2. Use your usual phone or computer. Stop if you get stuck; that is useful feedback.
3. [Share what happened](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new?template=experience.yml), including anything you expected to find but could not.

This is an unpaid, one-off activity for adult volunteers. You can skip a question or stop at any time. There is no purchase or website signup required. Some traditional stories include violence; choose material you are comfortable with.

GitHub requires an account to submit an issue. If someone invited you and you do not use GitHub, send your answers back through that existing conversation. With your permission, the organizer can record an anonymous summary for you.

## Something broken?

You can [report a website problem](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new?template=website-problem.yml) without completing the tasks.

## What this space is for

- Real experiences finding, reading or listening to stories, exploring cultural context, and obtaining the printable sample.
- Specific problems with navigation, accessibility, playback, downloads or appearance.
- Thoughtful suggestions linked to something you tried.

Reports are public, including your GitHub username and anything you attach. Please keep names, email addresses, passwords, account information and private browsing details out of reports. A screenshot is optional.

This repository contains feedback materials. Website code, unpublished work, artwork masters and recordings are not stored here. Sharing feedback does not give access to production systems or change rights to Folkloreverse content.

## Helping as a reviewer or organizer

Read [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md). The [volunteer invitation drafts](VOLUNTEER_INVITATION.md) are prepared for the owner to review and share where community rules allow.

Please be kind and specific. We are testing the website, not anyone's knowledge. Thank you for helping make the next visit better.
