# Contributing useful feedback

Thank you for taking the time to try Folkloreverse.

## Choose the right report

- [Share a two-task experience](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new?template=experience.yml): what you tried, chose and understood.
- [Report a website problem](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new?template=website-problem.yml): a broken or confusing interaction you can describe.
- For a broader suggestion, explain the visitor goal it would help with. You can include it with your experience report.

No code contribution, purchase or special software is expected. This feedback repository does not require or grant access to production materials.

## Keep reports useful and considerate

Describe what you actually observed, using ordinary language. A report that you got stuck is valuable. Do not invent results, quote another person's experience without permission, or present an AI simulation as a human test.

A page link and a few steps are usually enough. Screenshots are optional. Automated tool findings are welcome when identified as automated; they are not a substitute for a person's experience.

Keep the discussion respectful and focused on the website. Do not harass people, make personal attacks, share anyone's private information or post unrelated promotion. The maintainer may moderate material that breaks these expectations.

## Public information

Issues, comments, usernames and attachments are public. Avoid names, email addresses, account information, credentials, private links and uncropped screenshots. Do not post a recording of another person.

An organizer should obtain permission before publishing an anonymous summary of someone's session. Private participant notes belong with the organizer, not in this repository.

For a suspected security issue, avoid publishing an exploit, secret or private account detail. Contact the person who invited you through the existing conversation and ask for a private reporting route. Do not probe production systems as part of these tasks.

## How findings will be handled

The maintainer will review reports, ask a clarifying question when useful, and separate observed problems from suggested changes. Priority depends on whether the problem blocks a task, causes misleading understanding or adds avoidable effort—not on how many feature requests it generates.

Reports may be linked when they describe the same underlying problem. An issue is resolved when the relevant fix has been checked, not simply because a reply was posted. A report does not promise a feature or a response deadline.

There are no participant results yet at setup. Maintainers should record actual evidence as it arrives and keep skipped checks explicit.
