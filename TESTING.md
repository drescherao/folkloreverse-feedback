# Two short tasks

Choose **one pair** below based on what you would normally enjoy. Allow about 10–15 minutes in total. You do not need to finish a whole story or recording.

Start each task at [folkloreverse.com](https://folkloreverse.com/). Use the site as you normally would, including search if you wish. Please do not read other people's reports before your first attempt.

If something is unclear, tell us what you expected. If you get stuck, stop and note where. There is no need to find a workaround to give a useful answer.

## A · I enjoy reading stories

**A1.** You have about ten minutes to read. Find a complete tale you would choose and begin reading it.

**A2.** After looking at that tale, find something else on the website that you would genuinely choose next. Tell us what made it seem relevant.

## B · I enjoy listening

**B1.** You would like someone to tell you an Egyptian story while your hands are busy. Find a recording and start it when you are comfortable doing so. Listening briefly is enough.

**B2.** Pause and note roughly where you stopped. Leave that page, then try to return to that point using the same device and browser. Tell us what happened and what you expected.

You may skip playback if this is not a suitable time or place. Say what you could assess without playing it. There is no need to clear your browser data or change any privacy setting.

## C · I enjoy coloring

**C1.** You have an A4 or Letter printer. Find a page you could color this evening and work out which file you would use. Downloading or printing is optional.

**C2.** Find a story you would enjoy reading or hearing alongside that coloring page. Then tell us whether you think the full coloring book can be bought here yet, and what led you to that answer.

## D · I enjoy cultural context

**D1.** Someone mentioned Perun. Find what the website says about him and what evidence it gives.

**D2.** You want to tell a friend where the website's Isis story comes from. Find the account it follows and work out which part of the story this telling covers.

You do not need prior knowledge of either figure. We want to understand how the website explains its material.

## Send a short account of your attempt

Use [Share your experience](https://github.com/drescherao/folkloreverse-feedback/issues/new?template=experience.yml). For each task, tell us:

- Which task you tried and whether you reached a satisfying answer, got stuck or needed help.
- What you chose first, where you ended up, and what you expected to happen.
- Anything that made you hesitate or want to explore further.

Your device and browser are helpful if you know them. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first. Please describe only what actually happened. A skipped task is useful to record as skipped.

If an organizer gave you these tasks, you can reply through that existing conversation instead of creating a GitHub account.
